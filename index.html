About YNM GEMS

Located in Wuzhou, China, YNM GEMS stands as a leading professional manufacturer and wholesale B2B supplier of premium natural gemstones.

With over 15 years of master supply chain expertise, we specialize in the art of precision-cut, calibrated round gemstones ranging from 0.7mm to 5.0mm. Our advanced factory production enables us to meet the rigorous standards required by luxury watchmakers, micro-pave high jewelry brands, and major global distributors.

By bypassing agents and operating directly out of Wuzhou—the international heart of gemstone processing—we guarantee highly consistent size sorting, expert color grading, and competitive wholesale pricing tailored for sustainable B2B partnerships.

Gemstone Education

Calibrated Size Spectrum (0.7mm – 5.0mm)

  • Micro Size (0.7mm - 1.5mm): Engineered for ultra-fine micro-pave ring halos and luxury watch dial pavé.
  • Standard Melee (1.6mm - 2.5mm): The ideal industry choice for tennis bracelets, eternity bands, and side-stone settings.
  • Accent Size (2.6mm - 5.0mm): Perfect for clustered earring designs or standalone accents in contemporary jewelry lines.

Hardness & Durability Guide (Mohs Scale)

  • Sapphire & Ruby (Mohs 9): Exceptional scratch resistance, suitable for heavy daily wear and bridal jewelry.
  • Tsavorite & Garnet (Mohs 7 - 7.5): Excellent durability with brilliant refractive indexes, highly popular in luxury collections.
  • Topaz & Quartz (Mohs 7): Highly versatile with high clarity, offering premium aesthetics for commercial jewelry lines.
