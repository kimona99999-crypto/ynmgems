B2B Partnership Models

Tailored Commercial Logistics For Global Jewellery Manufacturing Houses

1. Wholesale Bulk Supply

Access our ready stock of 0.7mm - 3.0mm calibrated round stones. Optimized for distributors demanding immediate dispatch and high volume consistency.

2. OEM Custom Cutting

Provide your specific parameter profiles. We manipulate facet counts, girdle thickness variations, and raw parcel selections matching your dynamic blueprints.

3. Sample Order Packages

Low MOQ custom trial parcels curated specifically for scale verification, physical calibration matching, and corporate quality appraisal reviews.

4. Calibrated Sieve Sorting

Automated and manual mechanical sorting protocols guaranteeing absolute millimeter size precision for automated CNC or watch bezel pave layouts.

5. High Jewelry Single Orders

Dedicated tracking of unique clean color sets. Custom premium cutting for high-carat signature showpieces demanding intense color vibrancy.

6. Annual Supply Contracts

Secure locked-in wholesale unit rates and dedicated monthly production allocations. Perfect for luxury brands requiring stable supply chain visibility.
